## Tui 
An adaptation of the Thai game เลี๊ยบตุ่ย — a four‑player trick‑taking card game played with the pieces of Xiangqi (Chinese chess). 
You can play it on browser at [Tui](https://tui.peune.org/).

## I2G
A simple desktop app to transfer photos from iCloud to Google Drive.
- Download zip file from [I2G](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11Rwaq-HOY5_5sCvYSbAPa-Ip8rqwCaU7/view?usp=sharing)
- Unzip
- You should be able to run "iCloud to Google Drive.exe"
- Have fun
